Pore Pressure, Shear Stress &
Slip Failure Physics

Catastrophic landslides occur when intense monsoonal precipitation elevates subterranean pore-water pressure ($u$), drastically reducing normal effective stress ($\sigma_n - u$) along critical shear planes.

Mohr-Coulomb Failure Criterion
$\tau = c' + (\sigma_n - u) \tan\phi'$
When driving shear stress $\tau_{\text{driving}}$ exceeds available shear strength $\tau$, the slope undergoes rapid translational rupture or rotational slump.
