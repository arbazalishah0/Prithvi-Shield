THE UNSTABLE EARTH

Slope rupture occurs when gravitational driving shear stress exceeds the internal shear resistance of soil and weathered bedrock strata. Saturated mountain aquifers build hydrostatic pore pressure, reducing effective stress and triggering catastrophic landslides within minutes.

Mohr-Coulomb Failure Criterion
τ = c' + (σn - u) · tan(φ')
Where c' is effective soil cohesion, (σn - u) is effective normal stress, u is pore-water pressure, and φ' is the internal friction angle.
Factor of Safety (FoS) Stability Metric
FoS = Resisting Shear Strength / Driving Shear Stress
FoS > 1.3: Stable • 1.0 ≤ FoS ≤ 1.3: Critical Margin • FoS < 1.0: Immediate Rupture / Slide.
